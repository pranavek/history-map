Science & Technology of India

Mathematics, medicine, astronomy and engineering, from the Indus Valley to ISRO's current missions — a timeline, not a ranking, and every entry sourced. Where a popular claim outruns the evidence, this page says so rather than repeating it.

Every entry cites its source — click a heading's era to see the whole group, or a "Sources" line to see where a specific claim came from. Some framings here (a text "anticipating" calculus, a treatise "predicting" modern grammar theory) are real scholarly comparisons, not settled equivalence — the entries say so where that distinction matters.
